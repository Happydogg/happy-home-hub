Growth & Health Standards (生長與健康準則)

1. Pre-Heat Thermos (5 Minutes)

Always pour boiling water into the thermos and close the lid for 5 minutes before adding hot food so the lunch stays warm safely until lunchtime.

2. Bite-Sized Cuts

Kids eat with spoons at school. Cut all meat, ribs, and vegetables small so they can easily chew and finish their meal without struggling.

3. Avoid Excess Refined Sugar

Elly receives growth hormone therapy. Avoid adding refined sugar or sweet syrups to sauces. Rely on the natural sweetness of carrots, onions, and tomatoes.

4. 3:30 PM High-Protein Snack is Essential

Both kids are underweight. They must have a warm, nutrient-dense mini-meal (egg, cheese, yogurt, or dumplings) ready right when they arrive home from school.
